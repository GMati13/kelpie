# The story list

The sidebar's groups (In progress, reviews, your pull requests, To do, sunk, Done, Unassigned), what a story is, kept and reassigned stories, the order, priority, and when a story sinks.

## What a story is, and the order

A story is an issue of yours in the board's active sprints, or the parent of a subtask of yours, with all of its subtasks (`key in (…) OR parent in (…)`: the stories' own fields come from there too, as a subtask's embedded parent carries no assignee). On a board worked alone (`solo`, only `true`) every issue in scope is yours, whoever it's assigned to, or nobody (#91): Jira's search drops its assignee clause (`WHOSE` in board.js, two constants picked by the flag), a Project's load uses `KelpieProjectUserAll`/`KelpieProjectOrgAll` (filter `is:issue` instead of `assignee:@me is:issue`; past its 10 pages the answer has `cut`, the items read, and the list says under its freshness line that only that many were read, `stories-cut`), every issue found, kept stories too, goes into `mine`, and the load says `solo: true`, kept in the list's data; `build()` then counts every ticket as yours (`yours()`: the subtask groups, the progress bar, sinking, Move to Done, Next, reassigned never set), while the avatar's "(you)" (`you`, `assignedToYou()`) is left off, as such a load doesn't ask whose they are; the avatar still names who has it.

The demo's ACME-195 (Jordan Blake's, its subtask ACME-196 unassigned) and ACME-198 (unassigned) show only that way. A story stays while something of it is here even once the board stops giving it as yours (reassigned to someone else, or out of the sprint): `hereKeys()` (a space named after a key, a paused agent's key, a story with an open pull request of yours in the last load; ticket keys only) goes to `loadBoard(board, keep)`, which asks for those it didn't load (Jira `keptJira()`: `key in (…) OR parent in (…)`, asked beside the stories' own search rather than after it (#40: every key not yet known to be loaded, one the stories' children hold dropped after), only keys of the board's projects, a subtask's story too, each key alone when Jira refuses one (a 400: a key it doesn't know, skipped); yours those whose assignee is the signed-in account (jira.sh's `account`), else those Jira gives for `assignee = currentUser()`, a search more; `tests/js/jira-load.test.mjs` counts the round trips: 3 with kept stories, 4 when a kept subtask's story is asked too; a Project's `KelpieKept`, an issue GitHub says isn't there (`NOT_FOUND`) skipped, yours the viewer's) and lists them after the board's as `kept`.

Any other failure of those questions fails the whole load, so the list keeps its last data and `pruneShared()` doesn't drop the kept stories' paused agents. One with nothing of it yours is `reassigned` (`{to: [names]}`: its subtasks' assignees, else the story's): in progress, its row has the new assignee's avatar after its key (`gone-<KEY>`), its need line starts "Reassigned to <name> ·", it's cleanable (`letGo()`: finished, handed over or reassigned; `cleanBlockers()` doesn't ask it to be done, an open pull request still blocks), so its broom, Clean up all and Next's clean-up item offer it; its paused entries stay, as pruneShared() sees it listed. Once its space, paused agents and pull requests are gone it isn't asked for again.

The demo's ACME-190 is one. Pull requests (yours, open, plus merged in the last 14 days with a light query, about 2 s instead of 5; both, and the quick read, ask `mergedAt`) attach by the ticket key in their title; an agent counts for the key its tab is named after, else for its space's story. Order: what needs you (agent blocked, changes requested, checks failing, ready to merge) first, then the rest, then sunk stories, each in the board's rank (the `ORDER BY Rank` order `build()` keeps), but the sunk ones by when the work was handed over, the latest first (#141): what was just finished is what's checked next (`handed` on each story from `build()`: the latest move of your subtasks past the handover column, else the story's own; none known: after, in the board's rank), and the Done group by when it was done (`doneAt`, `byDone()`), each row's tooltip saying when (`whenWords()`: "Handed over 2h ago", "Done 5d ago", "about" when only guessed).

A ticket's move time is `movedOf()` (stories.js), kept as `fields.moved` `{at, exact}` in the shared copy: Jira's `statuscategorychangedate` for a done ticket (exact), else its `updated` (jira.sh's search asks for both; a move within one status category changes no category, so the last change is a guess); a Project's Status value's `updatedAt` (exact), else its `closedAt`, else the issue's `updatedAt` (a guess); a date that doesn't parse or lies in the future is none. In progress (not what needs you) goes by last activity, the latest first, never worked on here after in the board's rank (#141; `active` on each story, the latest of its and its subtasks'), its row's tooltip "Last worked on 12m ago": `activityOf()` takes it at each load into the data (`data.active`), so the list reorders only when it loads (the 2-minute timer, Refresh, Kelpie's own moves), never under the pointer as an agent finishes a turn; it counts what `noteActivity()` noted (shared `kelpie.stories.active` by profile, `{key: at}`, written by the copy that follows what's shown, `syncTracking()`: an agent's state changing, by its tab's key else its space's, first sightings not counted, and the story shown; at most once a minute per ticket, the newest 200 kept, pruned with the profile's listed keys), a pull request of it changing (`updatedAt`), a session on it writing, a paused agent's pause.

Within the rest, In progress always comes before To do, as drawn. PR-only rows come after the board's tickets. Priority (#122, `priorityOf()` in config.js, both off by default): the board's `byPriority` ("Order stories by priority", the Board page's Priority group) sorts To do by priority, highest first, then the board's rank, tickets with none after the lowest, and what needs you by urgency (`need()`'s rank), then priority among equally urgent, as Next's queue takes them; In progress and the sunk stories keep their order, as they're worked through by time (#220, #141) (`ordered(items, byPriority)`, To do then going after In progress within the rest); `showPriority` ("Show priority") marks a story row after its key (`prio-<KEY>`) and a subtask row in its key's column (`sub-prio-<KEY>`): `priorityLook()`, Kelpie's own arrows on either board (never chevrons: in Kelpie a chevron folds or opens something, so one beside a key read as a control), up for the levels above the middle (the highest an arrow to a line, in `working` amber, the only one in colour), down below it (dim; the lowest to a line), so each level differs by shape, not colour alone; none for the middle level or no priority, nothing red, the level's name its tip; the story header shows it beside its key too (`story-prio`).

Where it comes from: Jira's `priority` field (already in jira.sh's search) ranked by the site's levels, `/rest/api/3/priority` (`jiraPriorities()` in board.js, asked only while either setting is on; failing, no priority, never no stories); a Project's single-select field named by `project.priorityField` (asked by name as GraphQL variables `$priority`/`$withPriority`, `@include`d only while used), its options' order the levels'; none named, or a field the Project lacks: no priority. The load gives `priorities` [{id, name}], highest first, and each issue `fields.priority` {id, name} (`priorityLevel()` cleans both as a tag's name); `build()` gives each story and sub `priority` {name, rank, of} (`priorityRanks()`, by id, else name; never by guessing from names).

## Sinking and still talking

A story sinks when every subtask assigned to you is at or right of the handover column (`jira.handover`, "My part is done from column" in the profile dialog, a choice of the board's columns; unset: the first column matching /test|qa|verif/i, else the last, `handoverFrom()` in config.js); with no subtask of yours, and for a task without subtasks, by its own status. A story still being talked to doesn't sink (`sunk()`, `talking()`): while any agent on it is working, waiting for you or finished a turn you haven't seen (Herdr's `done`), or went quiet less than `STILL_FOR` (15 minutes) ago, it isn't sunk, and the sidebar lists it in a group of its own over In progress, "Still talking" (`stillTalking()`: handed over or done, nothing of it needing you; one whose agent waits for you is with what needs you), so In progress holds only work under way: its row drawn as finished (muted, with a Built row's actions, Move to Done among them), led by its agent's mark (`talkAgent()`: one that answered first) and saying on its key line "Agent replied", "Agent working" or "Talking" (`TALK_WORDS`), its tooltip why ("Its agent finished a turn", "Still talking to its agent"), its board status unchanged; a done story so stays out of the Done group (`settled()`). `agentsByKey()` marks such a key's agent `talking`; shared `kelpie.talking` notes by agent terminal 0 while seen live and when it went quiet after that (`settleTalking()`, run by `syncTracking()`, written only on a change), so one only ever seen quiet (a session left open from yesterday) doesn't bring its story back.

An agent's tab renamed after another key takes the conversation to that story. The demo's ACME-126 is one (its subtask in QA, its agent `done` in the fixture; Herdr starts the stand-in idle, so in a live demo it sinks until its agent is sent a message and plays a turn). Sunk rows are drawn muted in their own group, "Waiting on others", after To do; for someone who works alone (the board's `solo`, "I work alone" under the Board page's Your part, default off: a team) every ticket in scope is theirs, assigned to anyone or nobody (below), and nobody else has the sunk ones, so the group says "Built" (testing, in review or done alike, whatever the board's columns are called), `sunkWords()` in config.js, and What next?'s snapshot says they wait on the person to check.
