# Next and What next?

Next's queue, its card, Claude's picks and What next?, Next's pointer, and agents' asks.

## Next's queue and What next?

Next's queue and What next? (`needQueueOf()` in stories.js), in the order #252 agreed: what's blocked on a prompt (the stories ranked 0, then the Desk asking), the agents waiting on you (`waitingIn()`: their own asks and those found waiting by #251, `{key: "wait:<address>", wait, agent, it}`, one row per agent, the longest waiting first), your pull requests' needs (changes requested, failing checks, ready to merge), finished turns to check (`readyIn()`, `{key: "ready:<address>", ready, agent, it}`), the reviews for you, Ready for Done, Clean up all, then Claude's picks (`suggestedIn()`). A waiting or ready row's label is the agent's line, its detail the story's key and the agent's tab; a click shows the agent (in its story when it has one, `focusAgent()`) and leaves it waiting; its ✕ sets it aside until the agent's next turn (`setAside()`, `aside` on its entry); once its pane was focused (`seen`, noted by `noteTurns()`) it's drawn dimmer.

A story whose agent waits isn't a row of its own (it ranks `WAITING_RANK`, its agent's row stands for it). `get_next` reports them as `agent-waiting` and `agent-finished`. A notification, when the profile asks for it (`notify.waiting`, "Tell me when an agent waits on me" on the Agents page under Find agents waiting on you, off by default): `tellWaiting()` in triage.js as a turn is found waiting, once per turn, never for the agent whose pane is focused, the agent's line as its body, its click `kelpie.show-waiting-<id>` (shared `kelpie.waiting.show` maps the id to the agent for a day; `showWaiting()` in stories.js shows its space and pane, as show_agent does). Herdr's own "finished while you weren't looking" notification still comes too: Kelpie can't hold Herdr's back.

Next is one card (`nextCard()`), the whole queue's, so it reads as one section, the sidebar list's first item, scrolling with it (only the freshness line and the counts are pinned above: pinned too, a long open queue left the list no height and couldn't scroll itself, #125; opening the queue scrolls the list to its top, an empty first line `work-side-list-top`, as `scrollBox()`'s): a header line ("Next" and how many items the queue holds, as every group counts: a position, "3 of 3", read as another marker, disagreeing with the lit row; Claude's picks as a ✨ count badge that turns while Claude brews and opens the queue, the chevron) over, folded, the queue's most urgent item (its first; a pick of Claude's only when the queue holds nothing else), drawn as every queue row is (`queueRow()`: `queueMark()` the story's state mark, the broom, or sparkles; `queueLabel()` what to do; `queueDetail()` muted under it, the story's title, who asked for a review, or Claude's why; ↗ when the click opens the web, `queueAway()`; both lines cut to the row, its tooltip has them whole, `queueWords()`, folded Next's before where a click goes); folded on a pick of Claude's, the card is tinted; folded, that item has its ✕ when it's a pick or an agent's ask (`withDrop()`), as a queue of one has no open list to drop it from.

While that item is on screen its row is lit and says "You're on it", and where `cmd-shift-j` goes (the item after the one shown, `nextIn()`) is a smaller "Then … ⌘⇧J" line under it (`queueThen()`) whenever that's another item: the folded card is what most people read, so it never puts a pick in front of what needs you. A click goes to its item, and on a story points at its step (Next's pointer, below); the chevron unfolds the whole queue inside the same border (`queueList()`, per copy `this.queueOpen`; a queue of one has no chevron, its ✨ badge doesn't open it, and an open queue dropping to one item folds), in order, so Next goes to the row after the lit one (no copy on top, and no tag: a "Next" badge beside the header's "Next" only confused): Needs you, Waiting on you (in the `working` amber), Your pull requests, Ready for you, To review, Ready for Done and Clean up with counts (a label only when there's more than one group: alone it repeats the header), the item shown lit (not Next's, the one after it), then the picks as the card's last group, tinted, no border of their own (`suggestBox()`: "Suggested by Claude · <age>", each with its why and an ✕, `dismissSuggestion()`, a small Re-magic asking again, and with two picks or more, any startable, Run all beside it). (An earlier design drew the queue under the card, which read as separate sections.) A What next? run of the person's whose picks show (not all set aside) opens the queue once in every copy showing that profile, unless it's open already: shared `kelpie.next.picked` (by profile `{at, done}`, written by `brew()`'s `brewed()`, a day at most, as the picks last, a deleted profile's pruned by `pruneShared()`), read by `picksArrived()` each draw from the copy's first (`queueBorn`, so a card first drawn after "All clear" still opens), once per run per copy, only once some pick shows (the run isn't used up before), not for a run that ended before the copy was drawn (a reload) nor once the person folded that profile's queue after the run began (`queueFolded` by profile); another profile's opens when that profile next shows, and a queue opened so is that profile's alone (`queueOpenFor`: shown on another profile, it folds).

A run whose picks were all set aside as they arrived says so in a toast ("Nothing new to suggest", `brewed()`). Its top scrolls into view, as the chevron brings it: what needs you stays first, the picks under it (scrolling on to the picks put them in front of it on a short sidebar). Picks that came any other way, a failed run or one with none open nothing. While there are no picks, the magic button (`magic()`) is the card's last row, slim and quieter ("Conjure more to do", `{slim: true}`) so it doesn't outshout what needs you; with the queue empty, Next's place is "All clear: nothing needs you" over the full "Conjure what's next" (`allClear()`). It runs Claude in the background, no terminal, no Desk tab (`startSuggest()`/`brew()`, also `herdr-app do kelpie.what-next`, `whatNext()`): `claude -p` via `claudeArgv()` on the profile's account, in its folder, `--no-session-persistence`, `--output-format json`, `--permission-mode plan` (read only, whatever the person's default mode) and `--disallowedTools Edit Write NotebookEdit`, under `env KELPIE_PROFILE=<id>` so the plugin knows the profile (this Claude runs in no Herdr terminal); the task goes in on stdin, never as an argument, which `ps` shows every user (#26): `feedClaude()` writes it to `task` in a private `mktemp -d` folder and runs `stdin.sh <folder> <argv…>`, which opens it as the program's input and removes the file and folder before the program starts (exec takes no input; a run that never reaches stdin.sh removes them in `feedClaude()`, the file then the empty folder, never recursively); the task ends with `HAND_IN` (no `OTHERS`: the snapshot carries none of others' words), exec's timeout 10 minutes (`BREW_FOR`), so it ends on its own or is killed.

Motion is kept calm (a band sweeping over the content hurt to look at): what you read holds still, the shine runs only along the border, following its rounded corners (`borderGlint()`: a bright copy of the box's own rounded border seen through three nested windows sliding across it, each window moving one way while the copy inside moves the other with the same period and easing, so the copy stays put; on the magic button (6 s, 2.6 s brewing)). Its gotchas: a box clips inside its own border, so these boxes draw their border as an overlay (`edgeOf()`); `on_size_change` reports sizes as drawn (zoom included) while sizes set are zoomed again, so a 100-wide probe gives the zoom (`shineProbe()`, `shineSize()`) and the width is divided by it; the idle sparkles breathe slowly (`sparkle()`); brewing, the sparkles turn slowly with two bubbles drifting (`brewMark()`) and the words change every few seconds by `.frames` (`brewWords()`: `BREW_VERBS` × `BREW_THINGS` plus `BREW_ODD`, 262 phrases, shuffled once per run seeded by its start so every copy shows the same order; the small one cycles the verbs' first words); no sweeping bands over content, no fast blinking.

While it runs, shared `kelpie.next.brewing` (by profile, its start) gives every copy that look (Re-magic becomes the small words); a mark past the timeout no longer counts. A failure, or no picks, is a toast. Its message is `/kelpie:what-next <name>` with `board:`, `owner:` and the snapshot after a blank line (#246), unless the profile saved `prompts.suggest` (the person's own, else `/kelpie:what-next`, `suggestText()` in config.js, placeholders `SUGGEST_FIELDS`: `{name} {snapshot} {board} {owner}`; `{snapshot}` is `suggestSnapshot()`, in Kelpie's own words only (KELPIE-302): whether a sprint runs, each story's key and where it stands (to do, in progress, handed over, done), its priority as a number, what needs the person, whether it's open here and its agents' states, its subtasks by key (mine marked) and stage, pull requests by rebuilt page and state, reviews by page, how many agents the Desk has; no title, column, sprint, label or name).

The default is built for an unattended agent: the snapshot as a starting point (live Jira/GitHub win), a ranking ladder (finish my sprint work, help the sprint land, get ahead (the next planned sprint first when none runs, then the backlog), and only when those have nothing, make things better: 2-3 concrete improvements in repos under the folder worked in lately, as ideas; no lower rung while a higher one has something), 3-5 picks named as the sidebar shows them (a subtask by its story, one per story), each `{key | url | idea (+ where), action, why}`, read only. They come back through the plugin's `suggest_next` (`kelpie.suggest-next`, `suggestNextRequest()`, which runs `applyPicks()` and tells Claude what it kept and dropped, so it can fix those), never parsed from its answer: `HAND_IN` (config.js, after the profile's message whatever it says, as a message saved before asked for a JSON block) says so without naming the tool, and `brew()` counts a run that ends with no picks handed in since it started as a failure.

So What next? needs the plugin on the profile's account (`hasPlugin()`; without, `askPlugin(profile, "what-next")`'s dialog, as for Let me configure it for you; the demo needs none). `suggest_next` is marked read only (it changes only what Kelpie suggests), as plan mode refuses every tool not marked so, even one allowed by name (tested). It says what to look at, never how to reach or sign in to Jira or GitHub: the agent uses its own access (MCP servers, plugins, its settings' permissions) and skips what it can't reach; no commands, tokens or auth hints go in it. `applyPicks()` enforces the sidebar's rules (a subtask, by `data.children` or Jira's `issuetype.subtask`, becomes its story; a story's Jira `parent` is its epic and isn't followed; unknown keys dropped; one per story; PRs as github.com links, key `pr:<repo>#<n>`; ideas kept as they are, key `idea:<n>`, 3 at most, always after tickets and PRs; 8 at most) and replaces the profile's picks in shared `kelpie.next.suggested` (`{at, items}`, a day).

A pick drops out once its story is finished, sunk, already needs you or is in progress (`underWay()`: the person has it in hand, so the what-next routine asks for work not started, and Run all never starts a second agent on one), and stays out: `suggestedIn()` marks it `aside` (why, in words) in the saved picks, written only when one is newly set aside, and `applyPicks()` settles them against the list as they arrive, telling Claude which it set aside; those reasons can stop being true before the next load shows a story's new state (a pull request ready to merge, then merged), and the pick came back as if Claude had run again (#127). The "Suggested by Claude" heading counts them ("· 1 set aside", each with why in its tip).

A pick the list has selects its story; any other (unassigned, backlog, someone's PR) opens on the web, where you assign it; an idea (a lightbulb mark) starts a Claude on the Desk, tab `Idea: <action>`, with `prompts.idea` (`ASK_PROMPTS` `idea`, `{idea} {where} {why}`, the dialog's Messages "What next?" group; `startIdea()`, then `OTHERS`, as an idea may carry others' words) and leaves the queue; the default stays generic: the kind of work a person picks up (frontend, say) goes in their own prompt Run all (`askRunPicks()`): a `confirm` listing each pick and what happens to it (`picksPlan()`: a story of the list a new agent on the key picked, the subtask Claude named (`from`) else the story's own, as Start fresh, its space made when there's none; an idea a Claude on the Desk, `startIdea()`; one that only opens on the web left for the person, saying why), each startable one with a switch, all on (Confirm's `choose` items); only its own button starts them (Confirm's `runPicks`, `runPicks()`, through a run request, below under Overlays: never a command, as any agent can run one and this starts agents with Claude's words), the dialog closing at once.

One after another, the whole run under `kelpie.busy` `picks:<profile>` (Run all is then the working mark, saying which pick it's starting, and a second is refused) and `once("picks:<profile>")` and each story under its own `once()`; a story's first message is its ticket prompt, then `prompts.picked` (`ASK_PROMPTS` `picked`, `{action} {why}`, the Messages "What next?" group), then `OTHERS`. Each started leaves the queue; the note says "Started n of m: …" and names any that failed and why (a warning too).

## Next's pointer

Next's pointer (`POINT` in stories.js, shared `kelpie.point` by profile, `{key, kind, at}`): opening a story that needs you from Next (`goNext()`, so `cmd-shift-j` too) points at the step it needs (`setPoint()`, `POINT_KINDS` by `need()`'s rank: peek, comments, fix, merge; not for lone PRs or Claude's picks) and unfolds the details. While the story still needs that step (`pointFor()`, an hour at most) it's marked as a passage is (an accent tint, an accent edge), in one box over the details rather than tinted lines down the list (`pointBox()`, as wide as the subtask rows at most, `ROWS`, centred: Next, what to do in plain words, ✕, what its button does, then the pull requests it's about, each with its button, or, with more than one to merge, one Merge all ▾ for them, one ticket's or several's; the header's main action is then Merge all too).

The box fades in once, sliding 6px down (native `transition`s: drawn hidden, shown 30 ms later by `this.pointShown`), then a glint runs along its border twice to catch the eye (`borderGlint()`, as on Claude's picks, for `POINT_GLINT`, 5 s; `shineSize()`/`shineProbe()` now on Stories) and it rests; the details open at their top, where it is (`story-overview-<key>-<at>`, its scroll handle put back to the top once per pointing, `scrollBox()`); the header's button for the step is lit by a halo breathing slowly behind it (`story-pointed`, `.loop` opacity over 3.2 s). Nothing else moves. Taking the step (Peek, a PR button through `ask()`, a Merge/Request approval menu row), the ✕, or Next pointing elsewhere ends it; opened any other way, the story isn't marked.

## Agents' asks in Next

Agents' asks in Next (`ASKS` in stories.js, shared `kelpie.next.asks` by profile, a list of `{id, at, action, why, key, url, terminal, tab, rest}`): any Claude asks the person for something it needs (a choice, a plan to read, a PR to look at) with the plugin's `add_to_next`, which writes `<home>/kelpie/next-add.json` and runs `kelpie.add-next` (`addNextRequest()`, `addAsk()`). Its profile comes from the server's environment (`origin()` in server.py: `KELPIE_PROFILE`, else `HC_TERMINAL_ID`, the agent's terminal, whose space's profile counts, `requestProfile()`; else the shown one; a profile with no board has no Next, refused). Checked: action (a few words) and why cleaned of control characters and cut, the key against the key pattern (a subtask's becomes its story's), a link only `https://github.com/` or the profile's Jira site (`askLink()`: an agent can be misled into offering a page), a story's Jira page otherwise; three per agent (`ASK_EACH`, the same action replacing its last) and ten in all (`ASK_MAX`).

It only adds a line the person may take or drop, so nothing waits for a yes. In the queue it's `{key: "ask:<id>", ask, it}` after the stories that need you, under "Waiting on you" in the open queue (#252: one row per agent, its ask replacing a wait found for it), the waiting mark (`asks`), its action, its agent's tab name and why; a click (`goNext()`) drops it and shows its story (and focuses its agent when that's the story's), else opens its link, else focuses the agent; ✕ drops it too. It goes after a day (`ASK_FOR`), once its terminal no longer runs an agent, or once its agent was answered, however the person answered it (in its tab, without Next): seen at rest after the ask (done or idle, `RESTING`: its turn ended; noted as `rest`, at once when it already rests as it asks), then working again (`answered()`; a permission prompt, blocked, is the same turn, not a rest). `settleAsks()`, run by `syncTracking()` (the copy that follows what's shown) for every profile, notes and drops them, written only on a change; `asksIn()` hides an answered one meanwhile.
