# The Kelpie layout

The layout's blocks and roles, the tab bar's column, folding, split panes, keys and overlays.

## Blocks and folding

Our own replacement for the third-party Mission Control, built around profiles and stories. `layouts/Kelpie-Plain.json` (and `Kelpie.json`, the same tree skinned) is one flush surface (solid colours, no wallpaper, dividers instead of cards): `side` (`side-top` role `space-picker`, the profile picker; `side-list` role `work-side`; `side-foot` role `footer`), `work` (`story-head` role `stories`: the story's title row alone, 50px, not resizable; the column's top row while a story shows, so it moves the window (`window_drag`) where it has nothing to click, and the tab bar under it then keeps every press on its root (`topmost` in main.js `toolbar`), so neither it nor the details' divider just above it moves the window: `window_drag` is fixed per block, both tab bars have it, and only with no story (the title row hidden) does a tab bar drag; Herdr takes a press up to 3px either side of a divider's line as a drag of it, and while dragging puts the boundary itself under the pointer (measured on 0.1.15 and 0.1.16), so the tab bar sits in a column of its own (`tab-bar`, no divider inside) under a 3px strip that takes no press and moves no window (`tab-grip`, role `grip` in main.js, `GRIP` in work.js), which holds the grab's lower half: without it that half was the tab bar's, and the split often didn't drag (#276; before the tab bar kept its presses, it moved the window). Not a gap in the details column: the boundary Herdr drags is then the gap's top, which left the pointer on the grab's edge as it dragged and the cursor flickering. The strip shows only with the details (`syncStories()`), and the column is sized to what it holds, 45px or the tab bar's 42 (`TAB_BAR`), so folding leaves no band under the tab bar; their 4px side padding is the mod's, not the block's, so no edge of the block drags either (`tests/js/window-drag.test.mjs`); `story-body` role `story-body`: its note, status line, description and subtasks; `toolbar` with only Artifacts and Web on the right; `main-stack` = the terminals block `main`, the `story-view` block (Changes) or `story-shots` (Screenshots: kelpie-artifacts with props `{scope: "story"}`); `stackChild()` in stories.js maps a view to its child).

Folding (`kelpie.storyhead.folded`) hides `story-body`; the tab bar's chevron (`fold-terminals`, just before the side panels' icons, while a story with terminals or a view of them shows; no key, `kelpie.fold-terminals` for scripts) folds the terminals away instead (#271, shared `kelpie.terminals.folded`, `{pane}`: the pane focused then, `foldTerminals()` in stories.js): `lower` hides and `toolbar-start` shows, as for a story not started, so the details take the column and come back at the height they had (hidden, never resized); the chevron then points up, selected, its tip naming what's folded (terminals, changes, screenshots, artifacts, `FOLD_WHAT` in main.js). The two folds exclude each other (folding one unfolds the other; both left set, the details show), and going to a terminal unfolds: a view picked (`setView()`, so a tab's click too) or the focus on another pane than when folded (a new agent, Next, Show agent, Herdr's tab keys: `settleTerminalsFold()`, run by `syncStories()`, written only then).

Tests: `tests/js/fold-terminals.test.mjs`; details taller than their block fade out over its bottom edge (bands of the theme's background up to 90%, so a skin's lit background shows no seam), only while their content is taller than the view (`fits()` measures both, per copy, on `this.fit`), so text never just stops mid-line; a story not started hides `main-stack` instead and its details fill the column (folding ignored), until Changes or Screenshots is picked. `syncStories()` in work.js keeps all three in step, and the `panel` (Files, Changes, Artifacts, Web; Files and Changes only by key)

## Roles and the sidebar

Built from the `kelpie` mod's roles, no separate mod: `workside.js` (profile picker: a dropdown under the bar (`SpacePicker.menu()`, a Popup anchored to the whole bar), as wide as the sidebar's rows with its edges on theirs (`DROP`, incl. a measured 1.5px nudge for Herdr's popup offset), ↑/↓/Enter/Esc, each profile's waiting/working agents counted; its list has a set height, `menuHeight()` counted from its rows, 520 at most (`DROP.max`), as a scroll box with only a maximum grew with its rows and the entries at its end were out of reach; ↑/↓ scroll the lit row into view and opening it the shown profile's, `scroll_to_item` by its place among the list's children; each profile's spaces come from the one `spaceProfiles()` read, as `spacesOf()` per profile re-read every list and a long menu's render was interrupted; the sidebar: the stories of a profile with a board (Jira or a GitHub Project, `hasBoard()`), else "Working on" (a + in its header, and `cmd-n` (`kelpie.new-space`), opens a new space in the shown profile: `newSpace()` in config.js starts it where the shown space is when that's the profile's, else in the profile's folder, and notes the space as the profile's in shared `kelpie.space.profile`, so it stays the profile's whatever folder it works in): the profile's open spaces with their Claude agents by session name (double-click renames a space or an agent's tab, right-click opens Herdr's space menu), then "Earlier sessions" folded (its own Sessions source, which skips its timer's loads while the profile shown has a board, whose list loads the sessions itself: `wanted()` in source.js, a source not drawn loading at once when drawn again); the shown space's tabs are the tab bar's alone), `stories.js` (`Stories` = the title row, `StoryBody` = the details under it, `StoryList` = the sidebar list and the only loader, `StoryView` = changes, `storyNow()` for the tab bar and layout), main.js (`toolbar`, `footer`).

Older Hub roles (`hub`, `sprint`, `pulls`, `sessions`) are still in the code but not in the layout

## Split panes

Split panes: every Kelpie layout's `main` block has a pane `header` (32px), role `pane-head` (`panes.js`), drawn only while a tab is split (`lone: false`): an agent's pane leads with its state (`agentMark()`, as its tab), any other with its program's icon, then the agent's session name (else the terminal's title; a fresh shell titled with its terminal id shows the program), folder and branch; the focused pane's header is lit with an accent underline. Split right/down, zoom (lit while zoomed) and close show on the focused pane and on hover, using the icons of Herdr's shipped `pane` mod (`mods/pane/*.svg`, a real folder). Moving panes is Herdr's own top-edge grip. Dividers between splits are the pane frame's 1px `border` in the theme's `border` colour (gap 0): Herdr paints no background in the tab block, so a coloured gap can't be a divider.

A layout file edit isn't always noticed while it's shown: `layout.switch` away and back reloads it (and closes open windows)

## Keys

Keys (`keys.json` `layout:Kelpie` and `layout:Kelpie-Plain`; each `kelpie.<name>` reaches the shown toolbar copy's `nextCommand()` loop in main.js, which calls helpers exported from stories.js): `cmd-shift-j` next-need (cycles: the item after the shown one in `needQueue()`, what's blocked (the stories, then the Desk while one of its agents waits on a prompt), the agents waiting on you, your pull requests' needs, finished turns to check, the reviews for you, then one item for every story ready for Done (`{key: "finish", finish: [stories], column}`, below under Next's queue), then one item for every finished story with something to clean up (`{key: "clean", clean: [stories]}`: Next says "N finished stories: clean up all" and opens Clean up all's dialog, `askCleanAll()`, so it's one press), then Claude's picks; only with nothing at all does the What next? button take Next's place, wrapping; its space or preview, then its blocked agent's pane; the list's Next shows the same item; nothing there: any blocked agent, the shown profile's first, then another profile's, `goBlockedAgent()`), `kelpie.next-agent` (no key: Quiet Pages' footer safelight, which counts every blocked agent, every profile's, and beside it the agents waiting on you (#252, `waitingAgents()`, the waiting mark from `footerExtras`' `mark`, each count only when not 0, its tip "An agent needs you · 3 waiting on you: show the next"): its click shows the next of them after the focused pane, any profile's, blocked first (the shown profile's first), then the longest waiting (`goBlockedAgent()`, `nextAgent()`), never Next's other items, which once opened Clean up all (#250); the only one already focused stays, the mark saying "it's here"; its words and click both come from `attention.js` (`attention()`), `tests/js/attention.test.mjs`, `tests/js/waiting.test.mjs`; the same number, blocked plus waiting, is Herdr's Dock badge (#286, always on, nothing while none needs you: `syncBadge()` in main.js, run by `track()`, set only when it changes, and only where Herdr has `setBadge`, Herdr 0.1.16, reached through host.js (`canBadge()`, `import * as Herdr` there) so an older Herdr still loads the mod; `tests/js/badge.test.mjs`)), `cmd-shift-k` stories (the story picker), `cmd-shift-r` start (the header's main action: show its agent, else resume, else its latest session, else start), `cmd-alt-p` pause (the focused agent of the shown story, else the whole story when it has a space; a working agent, or a story with a working agent or agent-less tabs, needs a second press within 6 s), `cmd-alt-c` claude, `cmd-alt-t` terminal, `cmd-shift-h` fold, `cmd-alt-d` desk. `cmd-w` is `kelpie.close` in the Kelpie layouts (global `tab.close` elsewhere, since `kelpie.*` only reaches shown copies).

Clean up (`clean-story-<key>`), Clean up all and the Leave dialog's Pause (`pause-story-<key>`) run detached (`detach()` in main.js), as the loop handles one command at a time and a clean-up held every key until it ended. The loop ignores the same command twice within 150 ms (`lastCommand` in main.js, not shared state, which would redraw every copy), in case two toolbar copies are ever shown

## Agent order

Agent order: the story list (the loader) calls `setAgentOrder()` when its order changes, so `cmd-]`/`cmd-[` walk blocked agents of stories that need you first, then each story's agents in list order

## Overlays

Overlays (`dialogs.js`, roles registered in work.js): `story-picker` (popup at top; key, title, state line, cut to the row and whole in its tooltip; type to filter by key or words, the Done group's stories last, ↑/↓/Enter/Esc; a key hint line at its foot; the sidebar's search icon opens it for a profile with a board) and `confirm`. Closing a space's last tab (toolbar ✕ or `cmd-w`) opens `confirm`: for a story space "Leave KEY?" with Pause as the main button (runs `kelpie.pause-story-<key>`, the header's Pause path) and Close as the `alt` button (`tab.close {confirmed:true}`); other spaces just Close; other tabs close at once. A dialog asks, it never waits on a long run (KELPIE-262): a run that writes to the board or GitHub or starts agents (Move to Done, Ready for Done, Close, Run all) is asked for by the dialog's own button, which writes a one-time request to shared `kelpie.run.asks` (`askRun()` in stories.js: `{id: {kind, args, at}}`, its args checked: a profile id, ticket keys, a yes or no) and closes at once, as a closed dialog's tasks are cancelled; the copy that follows what's shown (`track()` in main.js) takes it as it draws (`takeRuns()`: removed, each id run once even if a draw sees it again, one older than a minute or malformed dropped unrun, the args checked again) and runs it in place, under its busy marks, ending in a note and a warning for what failed, as Clean up does.

Only that button writes the key: no `kelpie.*` command or plugin request does, as any agent can run one (`tests/js/run-asks.test.mjs`). Installing the plugin still waits in its dialog. Herdr's palette still uses its own dialog

## Peek

Peek: a blocked agent gets a Peek button (subtask row and story header) that runs `terminal.peek` to answer it without switching spaces
