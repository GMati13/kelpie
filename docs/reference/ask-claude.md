# Ask Claude

The Ask line, what it sends and to whom, right-click menus, and the story's tasks.

## Ask Claude

Ask Claude (`askclaude.js`, KELPIE-84): one way to ask a Claude about something Kelpie shows, meant to replace each place's own. A story's details (StoryBody) end with the Ask line, laid over the details' bottom (inside their view, so the room beside it on a wide screen scrolls the details; their content keeps that much room under it, measured, `askH`) and as wide as the subtask rows at most, centred (`askPart()`, `ROWS`), opaque and lit as the magic is (a faint accent tint on the page's background, a soft accent edge, rounded; still), over a lamp: from the details block's bottom edge up, the text under it darkened as the fade does and a warm accent light lit most at the bottom and fully see-through at its top (bands, reaching the block's own edges, so scrolling shows no edge of it), one row that never changes size (KELPIE-305, so nothing moves under the pointer): the sparkles, what it's about as faint words, the question (Enter sends; no Send button), ONE offer (`askOffer()`: the ask that fits what's happening, in Claude's voice: pull requests ready, merge?; subtasks done, catch you up?; nothing started, plan this first?; a file, lines or a passage, explain this?; else what's left?; a click asks it at once), a small ⌄ menu of every other ask (`askMoreRows`: the suggestions, the story's tasks, about what you copied), and who it goes to as that Claude's state mark and name (a menu; its sliding box as wide as the pill drawn, both forms measured by invisible copies, `askPill`, divided by the zoom, so no room is left at the line's end); writing (the cursor in it, `askAwake`, or something typed, `askTyped`), it makes room for the question: the offer and menu fold away, what it's about shrinks to a few letters, who it goes to to its mark, and a clear button comes (`ask-reset`: empties it and moves the cursor to the line's own focus handle, `askRest`, so it's back at rest), each sliding (width transitions, 220 ms); whether a Claude mid-task makes it wait is in who it goes to's tip.

Touched (the pointer over it, `askHover`) only its edge warms. Claude's presence is its motion: the sparkles breathe while it's touched, brew for `ASK_BREW` (2.6 s) after a send with a glint along its edge (`borderGlint()`), and a slow glint runs while the Claude picked works; nothing else moves: the sparkles, what it's about, the question (Enter sends), who it goes to (a menu, `ask-to`) and Send, then the suggestions for that kind of thing (a click sends one: `suggestionsFor()`, each a profile message, `ASK_CLAUDE` in config.js, the Messages page's Ask Claude group: a file's Explain this, Review this change, Fix this, Write a test for it; a story's or subtask's What's left?, Explain this; a pull request's Explain this, Review this change) and a word on where it goes.

What it's about is the story itself until something else is pointed at (shared `kelpie.ask.about` by profile, `{story, about, at}`, `pointAsk()`; another story shown, or the ✕, goes back to the story): a click on a subtask's title, "Ask Claude about this" in a subtask's or a pull request line's right-click menu (below), a file's Ask Claude in Changes (`fileAbout()`); Ask about what you copied, beside the suggestions (KELPIE-226: Herdr tells a mod nothing of a selection, but a mod can read the clipboard: its text, said to be from the story's description when it is, `textAbout()`, each line cleaned, `PASSAGE_MAX` 1500, fenced); a new pointing puts the cursor in the line once (`at`). What's sent is what it's about, written by Kelpie from checked values only (KELPIE-302: `ticketAbout()` for a story, subtask or pull request: its key and page (`ticketLink()`, or the pull request's rebuilt from its checked parts), for a story its subtasks' keys, the person's marked "mine", for a subtask its story's key; titles, statuses, assignees and descriptions stay in Kelpie, the label keeping the title, and Claude reads them itself; `fileAbout()`: a pull request's file by its pull request's page and its path when plain characters, else the person's own file's path and repo, its path on disk and whether it's committed on a branch with no pull request yet or not committed; all through `promptValue()`), then the question, then `OTHERS` only after a passage the person copied (the line then says "others' text").

Who: New Claude (in the story's space, made if needed) or one of the story's Claudes already open (`openClaudes()`: in its space or on one of its keys, never another story's or profile's; one waiting for the person listed but not pickable); existing Claudes first and picked by default, in the person's order (`rankOf()`, `askWho()`, `chosenWho()`): exactly the asked ticket's Claude, then one on the story's own ticket or one of its subtasks, then any other in the story's space, a new one last and only when none can take it (one waiting for the person never); the person's pick holds until something else is pointed at. `askClaude()` in stories.js sends it, and the person stays where they are (no space switch): an open Claude at rest gets it at once through `sendToClaude()`; one mid-task (`holdFor()`: working) isn't typed into, as that could steer its work: the question is held on the story's thread and goes once its turn ends (`releaseHeld()`, run by `syncTracking()` in the copy that follows what's shown, `releaseOf()`: sent once it rests, kept while it works or waits for the person's answer, given up with "its tab was closed" when it's gone); a new one gets the ticket prompt first, the question after it (that prompt ends with `OTHERS`), refused while the story's cleaned up.

No history of asks and no answers in Kelpie (the person's call, 2026-11-13): a short answer the Claude handed back (the plugin's `answer_ask`, `answerAskRequest()`, still there but no longer asked for: the message carries no `handBack()` line) read differently from its answer in the chat and misled, so the Claude's one answer is the one in its chat. Every ask is still kept on the story's thread (shared `kelpie.ask.thread` by profile then story key, the last `THREAD_KEEP` (12), none older than a day, `withEntry()`; it and `kelpie.ask.about` go with a deleted profile, `pruneShared()`, and with the demo), but only a question held for a Claude mid-task shows, a line over the Ask line with Send now and Cancel (`askThread()`), as that waits on the person; once sent, nothing of it stays in the story.

Right-click menus (KELPIE-225, its first part): a right-click on a subtask's row or a pull request line of the details opens that element's menu at the pointer (`elementMenu()`: a one-pixel anchor laid over the element where the right-click landed, so its layout is left as it is; `openMenu(id)` the right-click, its `local_position` divided by the zoom from `colProbe()`, `this.menuAt`; the same look and keys as `menuButton()`): Ask Claude about this first (it points the Ask line there and puts the cursor in it), then what it offers (a subtask: Open on the board or on GitHub, Copy link, Copy key, Show agent while one runs, `subMenuRows()`; a pull request: Open on GitHub, Copy link, `prMenuRows()`); their tips say "right-click for more".

Copy link and Copy key (KELPIE-239) are the same everywhere (`ticketRows()`, `pullRows()`): a link only when it's an http(s) one from the loaded data (`linkOf()`: the ticket's `ticketUrl()`, the pull request's `url`; any other, no Copy link), and a toast says what was copied ("Copied link to ACME-12", "Copied ACME-12", `copyLink()`/`copyKey()`). The title row has a menu too (a right-click on its key, title and state, `headMenuRows()`: Open, Copy link, Copy key; a lone pull request's Open on GitHub and Copy link), and so have the sidebar's pull request rows under Reviews for you and Your pull requests (`pullMenu()`: Open on GitHub, Copy link). In Changes a right-click on a line of code (KELPIE-227: the list's `on_item_secondary_click`, `lineHit`, says which line, the press's y in its item over `ROW` and the zoom; the box around the list where, for the menu at the pointer) offers Ask Claude about this line, about this change (its run of added and removed lines), then Copy the line (no range from the line right-clicked before: nothing marked it, so it couldn't be understood) (`lineMenuRows()`); what's asked about is `linesAbout()`: the file as `fileAbout()` says it, which lines, then, for the person's own file only, the lines with their numbers and +/−, each cleaned, `LINES_MAX` (80) at most, fenced; a pull request's lines aren't sent (anyone's code): Claude reads them there.

A story's tasks are the Ask line's (KELPIE-228): while it's about the story, Merge (while it has an open pull request) and Request approval (while one isn't approved, not for someone who works alone) sit beside its suggestions (`askTasks()`), sent to who the line has chosen: a new agent with the task (`startStoryTask()`) or the open Claude picked (`sendStoryTask()`), on the thread too (`askTask()`); the header no longer has their menus, the list's rows keep theirs. A story's row in the sidebar has its menu too (Ask Claude about this, which shows the story and puts the cursor in its Ask line; Open on the board; Copy link; Copy key; Show agent while one runs; `storyMenuRows()`, built only while open, as the list draws many rows), and so has a file's header in Changes (Ask Claude about this file, Copy path, Open on GitHub or Show in Finder; the list's `on_item_secondary_click` on a header item).

The menu's place is divided by whichever zoom the view measures (`colZoom`, the tags' `tagFit.zoom`, `diffZoom`). Not yet: Next's items and agents; buttons moved off the rows into the menus. Catch me up and Plan this (KELPIE-223, KELPIE-230) are a story's suggestions too (profile messages `catchup`, `plan`): Catch me up goes to a Claude already on the story when one can take it (a new one knows nothing of it yet), Plan this always to a new Claude started in plan mode (`claudeArgv(profile, "--permission-mode", "plan")`: it reads and changes nothing until approved); their entries carry `pin` (`brief`, `plan`), and the latest of each stays shown first on the thread whatever came after. A plan's answer may be longer (`PLAN_LINES` 40, `PLAN_MAX` 4000; the plugin takes up to 4500), and an answered plan has Go ahead, once, which tells its Claude the plan is approved and to build it a step at a time, stopping at each checkpoint (`sendToClaude()`; leaving plan mode is that Claude's own question, answered with Peek).
