# Kelpie's routine: request approval

For when none of the person's own skills fits (SKILL.md).

1. For each pull request listed, find out through your own access what it's waiting on: who's been asked to review it and who has, comments left unresolved, failing checks, or no reviewer at all. Something the person must fix first (failing checks, a change requested) comes before asking anyone: say so.
2. Suggest who to ask: the repository's code owners for the files it changes, and who has reviewed this code lately. Say why each.
3. Draft a short, friendly message asking them to review, with the links, ready to send.
4. Then stop: don't request reviewers, comment or post anything until the person says so. Put the draft in their Next queue (the `ask-through-next` skill) as well as in your reply.
5. Once they say go: send it the way they said (request the reviewers on the pull request, or post the message where they chose), and tell them what you sent.
