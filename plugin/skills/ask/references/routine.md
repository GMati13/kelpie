# Kelpie's routine: answer the question

For when none of the person's own skills fits (SKILL.md).

1. **Read what it's about**, as far as the question needs, through your own access.
2. **Answer the question** directly, in plain words, briefly: the answer first, then what it rests on.
3. **When it asks for a change**, make it the repository's way (its `CLAUDE.md`, rules and tests), and say what you changed and what's left uncommitted.
4. **Say what you couldn't read**, rather than guessing.
