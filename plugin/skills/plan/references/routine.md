# Kelpie's routine: plan this

For when none of the person's own skills fits (SKILL.md).

1. **Read** the ticket, its story and subtasks, through your own access, and the code it touches, the repository's way (its `CLAUDE.md` and rules).
2. **Write the plan**, in plain words:
   - the parts in order, each with what it looks like and how it behaves;
   - a checkpoint after each, where you stop for the person;
   - the open questions only the person can answer;
   - what could go wrong.
3. **Stop, and wait for the person's OK.** Ask through Next too. Once they approve, build it a step at a time, stopping at each checkpoint.
