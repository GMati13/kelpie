# Kelpie's routine: what's left?

For when none of the person's own skills fits (SKILL.md).

1. **Read where it stands**, through your own access: the ticket and its subtasks (their states, and whose they are), its pull requests (checks, reviews, whether they're merged), and any branch or uncommitted work for it in this folder.
2. **Say what's left** before it's done, and **what's in the way**: a failing check, a review waited on, a decision only the person can make, someone else's part.
3. **Keep it brief**: a few lines, the person's words, what waits on them first.
4. **Say what you couldn't read**, rather than guessing.
