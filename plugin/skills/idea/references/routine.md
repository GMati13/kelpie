# Kelpie's routine: look into an idea

For when none of the person's own skills fits (SKILL.md).

1. **Read where it points**: the code, its history, and open tickets or pull requests that touch it, through your own access.
2. **Check it holds**: is the problem real, and still there?
3. **Weigh it**: what it gains and for whom, how big it is (small, a day, several parts), and what it could break.
4. **Tell the person** in a few lines, with what you'd do, and ask whether to go ahead (through Next too). Change nothing until they say so; if they go ahead, a ticket for it may come first.
